## Business sellers

These five owners can be attached to any business of their type on the map. A business sale is priced mainly on the owner's yearly profit, plus the building if it is included. On the forms, switch to **Businesses** and use the Business Listing and Business Sale tabs.

**How a business is priced (RP guide)**

A small business usually sells for about 2 to 3 times the owner's yearly profit, plus the value of the building if the owner also owns it. Inventory is counted on the day before settlement and added at cost.

| Type of business | Leased space (business only) | Building included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Café or coffee shop | $60,000 to $150,000 | $300,000 to $500,000 |
| Restaurant or diner | $150,000 to $400,000 | $450,000 to $800,000 |
| Retail shop | $75,000 to $250,000 | $300,000 to $600,000 |
| Salon, barbershop or gym | $50,000 to $200,000 | $250,000 to $500,000 |
| Bar or nightlife | $200,000 to $500,000 | $500,000 to $1,000,000 |
| Auto repair garage | $150,000 to $350,000 | $450,000 to $850,000 |
| Convenience store with fuel | Rarely leased | $800,000 to $1,500,000 |
| Car dealership | Rarely leased | $2,000,000 and up |

These are RP figures, not market data.

**What is different from a home sale**

- **Commission** is usually higher: about 10% for smaller businesses and 6 to 8% for larger ones.
- **Confidentiality.** Owners rarely want staff or customers to know the business is for sale. Buyers sign a confidentiality agreement before seeing the books.
- **Owner financing.** Owners often finance 10 to 25% of the price themselves, and the buyer pays them back monthly.
- **Licenses.** Some licenses transfer and some do not. A liquor licence is transferred through the Pennsylvania Liquor Control Board. A lottery licence or a health permit usually has to be applied for by the new owner.
- **Handover.** The seller usually trains the buyer for a few weeks and agrees not to open a competing business nearby.

**Using a spare seller on a new listing**

1. Pick the spare that fits the house: the Brennan estate for an older home, or the Whitakers for a starter home.
2. Choose the street address and area, then take the town and ZIP from the table above.
3. Make up a parcel ID in the same style, for example 63-018-061.
4. Set the asking price from the price guide. Work out the seller's lowest price from their percentage: on a $300,000 house, 93% is $279,000 and 96% is $288,000.
5. Copy the rest of the seller's file onto the forms as written.

Use each seller on only one active listing at a time, so the story stays believable.
