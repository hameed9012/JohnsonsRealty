# Logo

The Johnson's Realty logo: the brass-framed roof-and-key mark alone or in a lockup with the wordmark.

- `variant="mark"` for favicons, avatars and the collapsed portal sidebar; omit it for the lockup.
- `tone="light"` on paper, `tone="dark"` on green or the portal theme. The roof flips between green and cream; frame and key stay brass.
- `small` for headers; `subline` replaces "Brokerage · Pennsylvania" (`false` removes it). The portal sidebar uses "Agent portal".
- `href` turns it into the home link.
- Never recolour, rotate or place it on a photo without a solid green or paper ground.
