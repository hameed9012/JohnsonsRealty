10 · Standard Terms
  1. Deposit. The Buyer shall pay the earnest money deposit as set out in Section 3. If it is not paid when due, the Seller may terminate this Agreement in writing.
  2. Contingencies. Each contingency is for the benefit of the party named and may be waived in writing by that party before its deadline. If a contingency is not satisfied or waived by its deadline, either party may terminate this Agreement in writing, and the deposit is returned to the Buyer.
  3. Title. The Seller shall convey good and marketable title by deed, free of liens and encumbrances except those agreed to in writing. The Buyer may obtain title insurance at the Buyer’s expense.
  4. Prorations. Real estate taxes, municipal charges, utilities and similar items are prorated to the settlement date.
  5. Condition of property. The Seller shall keep the property in its present condition until settlement, normal wear and tear excepted, including all included personal property.
  6. Risk of loss. The Seller bears the risk of loss from fire or other casualty until settlement and shall keep the property insured until then.
  7. Time. Time is of the essence. Dates in this Agreement may be changed only in writing, signed by both Buyer and Seller.
  8. Entire agreement. This Agreement, with any attached addenda, is the entire agreement between the parties. Electronic signatures and copies are binding.
  9. Professional advice. Johnson’s Realty does not provide legal, tax, financing, title or inspection advice. The parties are encouraged to consult qualified professionals before signing.

COMMISSION: Business sales usually carry a higher commission than home sales, because they take more work to market and close. It is paid from the owner’s proceeds at settlement, and shared with the buyer’s broker if there is one. No commission is owed if the business does not sell.
7 · Confidentiality & Showings
8 · Terms
  1. Exclusive right. The owner gives Johnson’s Realty the exclusive right to sell the business during the listing term, whoever finds the buyer.
  2. Authority. The owner confirms they own the business and its assets, or are authorised to sell them for the company named above.
  3. Financial information. The owner supplies the sales and profit figures. Johnson’s Realty passes them on but does not audit or guarantee them.
  4. Confidentiality. If ticked in Section 7, the brokerage will share the business’s name and financial records only with buyers who have signed a confidentiality agreement.
  5. Licenses. Some licenses and permits cannot be transferred, and a buyer may need to apply for new ones. The owner will help the buyer through that process.
  6. Running the business. The owner will keep running the business normally until settlement.
  7. Ending early. This contract may be ended before the listing term only by written agreement of both parties.
  8. Professional advice. Johnson’s Realty does not provide legal, tax or accounting advice. The owner is encouraged to consult an attorney and an accountant.

8 · Standard Terms
  1. Asset sale. The Buyer buys the assets listed above, not the Seller’s company. Debts and obligations from before settlement stay with the Seller unless this Agreement says otherwise.
  2. Financial statements. The Seller confirms that the sales and profit figures given to the Buyer are accurate to the best of their knowledge.
  3. Running the business. Until settlement, the Seller will run the business normally, keep stock at usual levels and not sell major equipment.
  4. Tax clearance. The Seller will obtain a bulk sale clearance certificate from the Pennsylvania Department of Revenue before settlement, so the Buyer does not take on the Seller’s unpaid state taxes.
  5. Prorations. Rent, utilities, property taxes and similar charges are split to the settlement date. Inventory is counted together the day before settlement and the price adjusted to the count.
  6. Risk of loss. The Seller bears the risk of loss or damage until settlement and will keep the business insured until then.

  8. Entire agreement. This Agreement, with any attached schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties. Electronic signatures and copies are binding.
